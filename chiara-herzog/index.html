Ask about Chiara M. S. Herzog's research

Answers are drawn only from Chiara M. S. Herzog's published papers listed on this page, with each point linked to its paper. They describe what the papers report, not personal views or advice from Chiara M. S. Herzog. This is not medical advice.

Try

DNA methylation biomarkers from accessible samples for cancer risk assessment and measurement of biological aging.

  • Uterine cancer detection. Contributed to the WID-qEC cervicovaginal methylation test for endometrial cancer (J Clin Oncol 2022) and its prospective comparison with sonography in women with abnormal uterine bleeding (Lancet Oncology 2023).
  • Cervical screening triage. Evaluated WID-qCIN methylation triage of HPV-positive samples in a real-world Stockholm screening cohort, where it predicted more incident precancers than cytology (Nature Medicine 2024).
  • Epigenetic risk signatures. Showed that a cervical cell methylation signature can identify women with ovarian cancer (Nature Communications 2022) and that smoking and e-cigarette use induce shared methylation changes linked to carcinogenesis (Cancer Research 2024).
  • Biomarkers of aging. Co-authored Biomarkers of Aging Consortium papers on the validation (Nature Medicine 2024) and systematic evaluation (Nature Aging 2025) of aging biomarkers.

  1. 1
    69% vs 18%of future cervical precancers (CIN2+) predicted by a methylation test plus HPV16/18, versus cytology

    In HPV-positive women from a real-world Stockholm screening programme, the WID-qCIN methylation test combined with HPV16/18 typing detected 93% of CIN3 and all invasive cervical cancers. It needed 2.4 colposcopy referrals per precancer found, compared with 4.1 for cytology.

    Schreiberhuber et al. 2024 · Nat MedReal-world screening cohort, 28,017 women (2,377 HPV-positive), 6-year follow-up
  2. 2
    92% vs 79%specificity of the WID-qEC swab test versus ultrasound for uterine cancer, at equal 91% sensitivity

    In women aged 45 and over with abnormal uterine bleeding at a London referral centre, a cervicovaginal methylation test found uterine cancers as well as ultrasound did but with far fewer false positives, doubling the positive predictive value (26% vs 12%). Triage with the test could reduce the number of women needing biopsies.

    Evans et al. 2023 · Lancet OncolProspective consecutive cohort (EPI-SURE), 399 women
  3. 3
    R = 0.12correlation between how well a clock predicts age and how well it predicts death

    Across 39 biomarkers of aging tested in more than 20,000 people, a clock's accuracy for chronological age said almost nothing about its ability to predict mortality. GrimAge2 was the strongest predictor of death and healthspan, so the best biomarker depends on the outcome of interest.

    Ying et al. 2025 · Nat AgingStandardised benchmark of 39 biomarkers (Biolearn) across cohorts of over 20,000 individuals
  4. 4
    22 yearsbefore diagnosis that smoking-linked methylation in cheek cells predicted lung cancer

    Smoking left 535 DNA methylation marks that differed by cell type and body site. Marks raised in cheek cells of smokers were also raised in lung cancer and predicted lung cancer up to 22 years ahead, and the same marks were raised in e-cigarette users with little smoking history.

    Herzog et al. 2024 · Cancer ResMethylation analysis of over 3,500 cheek/saliva, cervical and blood samples
  5. 5
    Shared termsand a framework for classifying and validating biomarkers of aging

    The Biomarkers of Aging Consortium set out shared terms and properties for biomarkers of aging, classified them by type and potential clinical use, and outlined the validation steps needed before they can be used to evaluate longevity interventions in humans.

    Moqri et al. 2023 · CellConsensus review, Biomarkers of Aging Consortium (28 authors)
  6. 6
    No consensusyet on how omic biomarkers of aging should be validated before clinical use

    Reviewing how omic aging biomarkers have been tested in population studies, the authors found major gaps in comparability and generalisability and gave recommendations for systematic validation, so that biomarkers could become surrogate endpoints in gerotherapeutic trials.

    Moqri et al. 2024 · Nat MedReview with recommendations, Biomarkers of Aging Consortium
  7. 7
    6 barriersto bringing biomarkers of aging into the clinic

    A survey of experts identified six key barriers to clinical translation of aging biomarkers. Core recommendations were to link biomarkers to actionable clinical decisions, make them affordable and widely available, and validate that they are robust and responsive in individual people.

    Biomarkers of Aging Consortium et al. 2024 · Nat AgingExpert survey and consensus recommendations
  8. 8
    97%sensitivity of a cervical swab methylation test for endometrial cancer in symptomatic women

    The WID-qEC test, which measures methylation in two genes (GYPC and ZSCAN12), detected endometrial cancer in 90 to 100% of cases across smear, vaginal swab and self-collected samples, and found 91% of cancers in samples taken up to a year before diagnosis. Because it works on self-samples, it could cut in-person visits.

    Herzog et al. 2022 · J Clin OncolDevelopment in 726 samples, validation in 562 samples across four clinical settings
  9. 9
    AUC 0.98for the WID-qEC test versus 0.56 for ultrasound in Ghanaian women with abnormal bleeding

    In Black women aged 40 and over with abnormal uterine bleeding in Ghana, ultrasound endometrial thickness barely distinguished cancer from no cancer, while the methylation swab test detected every endometrial and cervical cancer.

    Ken-Amoah et al. 2025 · Int J CancerProspective observational cohort, 102 women with biopsy
  10. 10
    OR 2.7for breast cancer in premenopausal women with a lower epithelial-versus-general clock gap

    Herzog's team built cervical-sample epigenetic clocks for epithelial cells, immune cells and both together. A smaller gap between epithelial and general age was linked to breast cancer in premenopausal women and was reversed by anti-progesterone drugs, suggesting differing clock tick rates can signal disease risk.

    Barrett et al. 2022 · Genome BiolClock development in 1,941 cervical samples, plus breast tissue datasets
  11. 11
    64%of BRCA1/2 carriers whose breast tissue showed reduced mitotic age on mifepristone

    BRCA1/2 carriers had higher progesterone levels across the cycle. The anti-progesterone mifepristone lowered the epigenetic mitotic age and the share of luminal progenitor cells in normal breast tissue of all control women and 64% of carriers, and reduced TP53 mutations in responders, supporting antiprogestins for breast cancer prevention.

    Bartlett et al. 2022 · Genome MedBreast tissue from three clinical trials of antiprogestins (n = 44) plus tissue and saliva studies
  12. 12
    AUC 0.84for identifying women with breast cancer from methylation in a cervical sample

    A methylation index read from cervical smear cells (WID-BC-index) identified women with breast cancer, with an AUC of 0.81 in external validation. Progesterone-receptor sites with low methylation in breast tissue of women with breast cancer were also low in their cervical cells, pointing to a body-wide epigenetic defect.

    Barrett et al. 2022 · Nat Commun2,818 cervical, 357 cheek, 227 blood and 42 breast tissue samples
  13. 13
    AUC 0.76for detecting ovarian cancer from cervical cells, without any tumour DNA

    A methylation signature in cervical cells, which share their embryonic origin with most ovarian cancer tissue, identified women with ovarian cancer and endometrial cancer (AUC 0.81). The signature mirrored the epigenetic programme of at-risk tissues in BRCA1/2 carriers, suggesting cervical cells report cancer predisposition.

    Barrett et al. 2022 · Nat CommunCase-control methylation study of cervical samples
  14. 14
    0.75 / 0.66 / 0.51validation AUCs for breast cancer detection from cheek, cervical and blood methylation

    Comparing three non-invasive sample types, cheek and cervical cells carried breast cancer methylation signals while blood carried essentially none. Cheek cell changes aligned most closely with changes in breast tissue itself.

    Herzog et al. 2025 · Nat CommunMethylation profiling of 1,100 cervical, cheek and blood samples from cases and controls
  15. 15
    9.6-foldhigher breast cancer risk for women high on both genetic and methylation risk scores

    Methylation at 104 breast cancer-linked genetic loci (WID-qtBC index) detected breast cancer from cervical samples. It changed with age, body mass index and a preventive drug, so it captures non-inherited risk; combined with a polygenic score it identified a top group with 9.6 times higher risk.

    Herzog et al. 2023 · NPJ Precis OncolMethylation in 1,848 samples and genotypes in 1,442 women
  16. 16
    >50%reduction in mammary cancer risk with mifepristone in mice

    In a hormone- and carcinogen-induced mouse model, mifepristone more than halved mammary cancer risk. The drop was matched by declines in methylation signatures both in the breast and in distant tissues such as the cervix, supporting these as markers of whether prevention is working.

    Barrett et al. 2025 · Commun Med (Lond)Mouse carcinogenesis model with multi-tissue methylation profiling
  17. 17
    Discordantepigenetic aging in breast cancer, faster in the tumour and slower in some distant tissues

    Grouping age-related methylation changes by function (senescence, stem-cell genes, proliferation) across more than 12,500 human and 105 mouse samples showed accelerated aging in breast cancer tissue but slower epigenetic aging in cervical samples from the same patients. It also flagged sites that change earliest in carcinogenesis.

    Herzog et al. 2025 · Commun Med (Lond)Multi-organ methylation analysis, over 12,510 human and 105 mouse samples
  18. 18
    52%of women with a normal smear who went on to develop CIN3 within 1 to 4 years were flagged by WID-qCIN

    The three-gene WID-qCIN methylation test detected all invasive cervical cancers and 78% of CIN3 at 90% specificity, and flagged half of women aged 30 or over whose cytology was normal but who later developed CIN3.

    Herzog et al. 2022 · Clin EpigeneticsDiagnostic and nested case-control validation, 761 samples
  19. 19
    AUC 0.92for the WID-EC test identifying endometrial cancer in an external validation set

    A 500-site methylation signature read from cervical screening samples identified women with endometrial cancer and, in samples from routine Swedish screening, picked up women who developed the cancer within 3 years with 98% specificity.

    Barrett et al. 2023 · Int J CancerDiscovery in 1,086 women; external (289) and prospective (150) validation
  20. 20
    94%sensitivity for high-risk ovarian cancer when blood cfDNA methylation was combined with CA125

    Targeted methylation of cell-free DNA in plasma detected 80% of high-risk ovarian cancers at 98% specificity, rising to 94% with CA125. In archived screening-trial samples it caught some cancers up to a year before diagnosis.

    Herzog et al. 2024 · Int J CancerDiagnostic case-control (68) and screening-trial nested case-control (58) samples
  21. 21
    OR 2.93for ovarian cancer among women with a positive WID-qEC test but no uterine cancer

    Among women without endometrial or cervical cancer, 54% of positive WID-qEC results came from women with ovarian cancer, and positivity was also linked to more lifetime ovulatory cycles. A positive test without uterine cancer may therefore warrant ovarian follow-up.

    Redl et al. 2026 · Int J Cancer1,269 cervico-vaginal samples (624 healthy, 324 benign, 321 ovarian cancer)
  22. 22
    No oncogenic changein the endometrium of BRCA carriers after 3 months of mifepristone

    In a randomised placebo-controlled trial, mifepristone stopped periods in all treated BRCA1/2 carriers without increasing epithelial cells or shifting methylation and expression markers of endometrial cancer, easing a safety concern for antiprogestin prevention.

    Widschwendter et al. 2026 · Commun Med (Lond)Secondary analysis of a randomised controlled trial, 45 women (14 with paired biopsies)
  23. 23
    ρ = -0.43correlation between an HPV-response methylation signature and replicative age of cervical cells

    High-risk HPV infection leaves a methylation signature in healthy cervical cells that is raised in mild lesions but absent in precancer and cancer. It tracks apoptosis and falls with replicative age, suggesting older cells lose the clearance response and progress to cancer.

    Herzog et al. 2023 · Int J CancerCervical methylation array study across the HPV disease spectrum
  24. 24
    Low-intensity probesare the least reliable on Illumina methylation arrays, including the newer EPIC v2

    Probes with low signal intensity gave the most variable methylation readings between repeated samples. The team built a method and R package to score and exclude unreliable probes, which matters for any clock built on these arrays.

    Nazarenko et al. 2024 · Clin EpigeneticsPaired blood samples six weeks apart plus technical replicates across EPIC and 450k arrays
  25. 25
    5 criteriato judge immune aging biomarkers for clinical trials

    There is no consensus on measuring immune aging. Applying five evaluation criteria to candidates, multidimensional immune function scores, inflammaging scores and functional assays performed best, in the context of the XPRIZE Healthspan competition.

    Cipriano et al. 2026 · Nat MedConsensus review and evaluation framework
  26. 26
    8 systemsof the body where digital biomarkers of ageing were found

    A review of wearable and other digital measures found biomarkers of ageing across eight of ten physiological systems, rated for validity, generalisability, response to interventions and cost, and listed trials already using them as outcomes.

    Lu et al. 2025 · Lancet Healthy LongevNarrative review with systematic search
  27. 27
    Minutesfor microglia to reach a brain injury and start clearing dead cells

    In live-imaged zebrafish larvae, microglia arrived at a brain lesion within minutes and engulfed dead cells. Slowing this clearance increased secondary neuronal death, showing that microglial debris removal protects the brain after injury.

    Herzog et al. 2019 · DevelopmentIn vivo live imaging in larval zebrafish brain injury model
