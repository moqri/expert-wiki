Key work

Clinical trials of testosterone and other function-promoting therapies on muscle, physical function and safety in men.

  • Androgen dose-response. Showed that testosterone increases muscle size and strength in a dose-dependent manner (N Engl J Med 1996), and that older men respond to graded testosterone doses similarly to young men (J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2005).
  • Cardiovascular safety. Reported excess cardiovascular events in a trial of testosterone in older men with limited mobility (N Engl J Med 2010); the larger TRAVERSE trial found no increase in major adverse cardiac events (N Engl J Med 2023).
  • Diagnosis and guidelines. Led the Endocrine Society clinical practice guideline on testosterone therapy in men with hypogonadism (J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2018).
  • Sarcopenia. Contributed to international consensus definitions of sarcopenia (J Am Med Dir Assoc 2011; J Am Geriatr Soc 2020).

    7.0% vs 7.3%of men had a major heart event on testosterone vs placebo

    In men aged 45 to 80 with low testosterone and existing or high risk of heart disease, testosterone gel did not increase heart attacks, strokes or cardiovascular deaths over about 3 years compared with placebo. Atrial fibrillation, acute kidney injury and blood clots in the lungs were more common with testosterone.

    Lincoff et al. 2023 · N Engl J MedRandomized placebo-controlled trial (TRAVERSE), 5,246 men

    0.19% vs 0.12%of men developed high-grade prostate cancer on testosterone vs placebo, not a significant difference

    In carefully screened men with low testosterone, high-grade prostate cancer, any prostate cancer, urinary retention and prostate procedures were rare and did not differ significantly between testosterone and placebo. PSA rose more with testosterone.

    Bhasin et al. 2023 · JAMA Netw OpenRandomized placebo-controlled trial (TRAVERSE), 5,204 men
  3. 3
    264-916 ng/dLis the normal testosterone range for healthy non-obese men aged 19 to 39

    Testosterone measured by mass spectrometry in four large community cohorts in the United States and Europe was cross-calibrated to one reference standard, giving a harmonized normal range that different laboratories can share.

    Travison et al. 2017 · J Clin Endocrinol MetabFour community cohort studies, 9,054 men
  4. 4
    Symptoms + 2 testsare required before treating low testosterone

    The Endocrine Society guideline recommends diagnosing testosterone deficiency only in men who have both consistent symptoms and clearly low morning testosterone confirmed on a repeat fasting measurement.

    Bhasin et al. 2018 · J Clin Endocrinol MetabClinical practice guideline, evidence graded with GRADE
  5. 5
    +3.4 to +7.9 kgof lean mass gained as the weekly testosterone dose rose from 125 mg to 600 mg

    With their own testosterone suppressed, men receiving graded weekly doses for 20 weeks gained lean mass, thigh muscle volume, leg strength and hemoglobin in proportion to the testosterone level reached, while fat mass fell.

    Bhasin et al. 2001 · Am J Physiol Endocrinol MetabRandomized dose-response trial, 61 healthy men aged 18 to 35
  6. 6
    As responsiveolder men are to testosterone's muscle effects as young men

    Older men aged 60 to 75 given the same graded doses as young men gained lean mass and strength to a similar degree, so muscle loss with age is not explained by muscle becoming less sensitive to testosterone. Their hemoglobin rose more than in young men.

    Bhasin et al. 2005 · J Clin Endocrinol MetabRandomized dose-response trial, 60 older men, compared with 61 young men
  7. 7
    6.1 kgof lean mass gained with testosterone plus weight training in 10 weeks

    Healthy men given high-dose testosterone gained muscle size and strength even without exercise, and the largest gains came from combining testosterone with supervised weight training. Mood and behavior did not change.

    Bhasin et al. 1996 · N Engl J MedRandomized placebo-controlled trial, 43 healthy men
  8. 8
    No benefitof testosterone for preventing diabetes in men with prediabetes

    Among men with low testosterone and prediabetes, the share progressing to diabetes was no lower with testosterone than placebo at every point up to 4 years, and blood sugar control did not improve in men who already had diabetes.

    Bhasin et al. 2024 · JAMA Intern MedRandomized placebo-controlled substudy (TRAVERSE), 1,175 men with prediabetes and 3,880 with diabetes
  9. 9
    3.50% vs 2.46%of men had a fracture on testosterone vs placebo

    Although testosterone improves bone density, men treated for a median of 3.2 years had no fewer fractures than men on placebo, and the fracture rate was numerically higher.

    Snyder et al. 2024 · N Engl J MedRandomized placebo-controlled substudy (TRAVERSE), 5,204 men
  10. 10
    41% vs 28%of anemic men had their anemia corrected at 6 months on testosterone vs placebo

    In men with low testosterone and anemia, testosterone corrected the anemia more often than placebo at every point through 4 years, and fewer men without anemia developed it. Rising hemoglobin tracked with better energy.

    Pencina et al. 2023 · JAMA Netw OpenRandomized placebo-controlled substudy (TRAVERSE), 815 men with anemia and 4,379 without
  11. 11
    +0.49sexual acts per day with testosterone vs placebo at 6 months

    Men with low testosterone and low libido reported more sexual activity, stronger desire and fewer symptoms of low testosterone, with the effect still present at 2 years. Erectile function did not improve.

    Pencina et al. 2024 · J Clin Endocrinol MetabRandomized placebo-controlled substudy (TRAVERSE), 1,161 men with low libido
  12. 12
    23 vs 5men had cardiovascular adverse events on testosterone vs placebo

    A trial of testosterone gel in older men with limited mobility and a high burden of chronic disease was stopped early because of more cardiovascular events in the testosterone group, even though leg and chest strength and stair climbing improved.

    Basaria et al. 2010 · N Engl J MedRandomized placebo-controlled trial halted early, 209 men aged 65 and over
  13. 13
    20.5% vs 12.6%of older men improved their 6-minute walk distance by 50 m or more

    In men aged 65 and over with low testosterone, a year of testosterone gel improved sexual activity, desire and erectile function, modestly improved walking across the whole trial population, and did not improve vitality.

    Snyder et al. 2016 · N Engl J MedRandomized placebo-controlled trials (The Testosterone Trials), 790 men
  14. 14
    No added musclefrom eating more protein than the recommended allowance

    Older men with physical limitations who ate 1.3 g/kg/day of protein gained no more lean mass, strength, physical function or well-being than men eating the recommended 0.8 g/kg/day, and the extra protein did not amplify testosterone's effect. Fat mass fell with higher protein.

    Bhasin et al. 2018 · JAMA Intern MedRandomized controlled feeding trial, 92 men aged 65 and over
  15. 15
    52%of products sold online as androgen receptor modulators actually contained one

    Of 44 products marketed over the internet as selective androgen receptor modulators, fewer than half contained one, 39% contained a different unapproved drug, and the labeled amount matched the analyzed content in only 41%.

    Van Wagoner et al. 2017 · JAMAChemical analysis of 44 products purchased online
  16. 16
    Not neededis conversion to dihydrotestosterone for testosterone to build muscle

    Men given graded testosterone doses together with dutasteride, which blocks conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone, gained just as much lean mass as men given testosterone alone, and sexual function, prostate volume and blood counts were also unaffected.

    Bhasin et al. 2012 · JAMARandomized placebo-controlled trial, 139 healthy men aged 18 to 50
  17. 17
    2.5% to 15%rise in muscle satellite cells on the highest testosterone dose

    Muscle biopsies showed that testosterone-driven fiber growth comes with more muscle stem (satellite) cells and more myonuclei, in proportion to the dose and the testosterone level reached.

    Sinha-Hikim et al. 2003 · Am J Physiol Endocrinol MetabMuscle biopsy substudy of a randomized dose-response trial
  18. 18
    Within 1 weektestosterone suppresses hepcidin, the hormone that limits iron supply

    High testosterone doses rapidly lowered hepcidin, and the fall was greater in older men, matching their larger rise in hemoglobin. This offers a mechanism for the rise in red cells during testosterone treatment.

    Bachman et al. 2010 · J Clin Endocrinol MetabDose-response study, 53 young and 56 older men
  19. 19
    7% to 10%rise in hemoglobin and hematocrit with testosterone

    Testosterone raised erythropoietin and lowered ferritin and hepcidin, and erythropoietin stayed high even once hemoglobin had risen, suggesting testosterone resets the relationship between the two.

    Bachman et al. 2014 · J Gerontol A Biol Sci Med SciRandomized placebo-controlled trial in older men with mobility limitation
  20. 20
    No changein carotid thickening or coronary calcium over 3 years of testosterone

    In men aged 60 and over with low or low-normal testosterone, three years of testosterone gel did not change the progression of atherosclerosis measured two ways, and did not improve sexual function or quality of life. The trial was not powered for clinical heart events.

    Basaria et al. 2015 · JAMARandomized placebo-controlled trial (TEAAM), 308 men
  21. 21
    3.7xthe odds of a hematocrit above 50% with testosterone

    Pooling the randomized trials available by 2004 showed that the most common adverse effect of testosterone in older men was a rise in red cell concentration, along with more prostate events detected, supporting routine monitoring of blood counts and PSA.

    Calof et al. 2005 · J Gerontol A Biol Sci Med SciMeta-analysis of 19 randomized trials, 1,084 men
  22. 22
    35 trialspooled found no excess cardiovascular risk with testosterone

    Combining individual participant data from 17 randomized trials with aggregate data from 18 more found no evidence that testosterone treatment raised cardiovascular or death risk in men with low testosterone, although most trials were short.

    Hudson et al. 2022 · Lancet Healthy LongevIndividual-participant meta-analysis, 35 trials, 5,601 men
  23. 23
    No recurrenceof prostate cancer during 12 weeks of testosterone after prostatectomy

    In men with low testosterone after surgery for low-grade prostate cancer, testosterone improved sexual activity, desire, well-being, body composition and aerobic capacity with no biochemical recurrence. The trial was too short and small to settle long-term safety.

    Bhasin et al. 2026 · JAMA Intern MedRandomized placebo-controlled phase 2 trial, 136 men
  24. 24
    Lean mass upon an oral selective androgen receptor modulator, with no PSA change

    Three weeks of the oral compound LGD-4033 increased lean body mass in healthy young men without changing PSA, liver tests or blood counts, while lowering their own testosterone, SHBG and HDL cholesterol in a dose-dependent way.

    Basaria et al. 2013 · J Gerontol A Biol Sci Med SciRandomized placebo-controlled trial, 76 healthy men aged 21 to 50
  25. 25
    2.3 kgof lean mass gained by men with HIV and weight loss given testosterone

    In men with HIV, weight loss and low testosterone, testosterone alone, resistance exercise alone, and the two combined each increased muscle mass and strength; combining them was no better than either on its own.

    Bhasin et al. 2000 · JAMARandomized placebo-controlled trial, 61 men
  26. 26
    Modest gainsin walking distance and self-reported mobility with testosterone

    In older men with low testosterone and mobility limitation, testosterone consistently improved self-reported walking ability and modestly increased 6-minute walk distance, with larger effects in men who started slower. Falls were identical in both groups.

    Bhasin et al. 2018 · Lancet Diabetes EndocrinolSecondary analysis of randomized trials (The Testosterone Trials), 790 men
  27. 27
    348 ng/dLis the lower limit of normal testosterone in healthy young men

    Mass spectrometry in a community sample of healthy non-obese men aged 19 to 40 set a reference range, and men below it in three other cohorts more often had slow walking, difficulty climbing stairs, frailty, diabetes or sexual symptoms.

    Bhasin et al. 2011 · J Clin Endocrinol MetabFramingham Heart Study reference sample of 456 men, applied to three cohorts
  28. 28
    Mood and energyimprove modestly with testosterone, but cognition and sleep do not

    Half the men entering the TRAVERSE trial had significant depressive symptoms, but only 1.5% met strict criteria for persistent depressive disorder. Testosterone modestly improved mood and energy overall, with no effect on cognition or sleep quality.

    Bhasin et al. 2024 · J Clin Endocrinol MetabRandomized placebo-controlled substudy (TRAVERSE), 5,204 men
