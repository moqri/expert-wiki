Roles of APOE, sleep and immune cells in amyloid and tau pathology in Alzheimer's disease.

  • APOE and amyloid. Showed in mouse models that APOE isoform influences amyloid deposition, and that human APOE isoforms differ in clearance of amyloid-β from the brain rather than in its production.
  • APOE and tau. Found that APOE4 markedly exacerbates tau-mediated neurodegeneration in a tauopathy model independently of amyloid, and that selective removal of astrocytic APOE4 strongly protects against it.
  • Sleep-wake cycle. In vivo microdialysis showed that brain interstitial amyloid-β rises with wakefulness and sleep deprivation; sleep loss similarly increased tau in mouse brain fluid and human CSF.
  • Immune mechanisms and therapies. In tauopathy mice, microglia-mediated T cell infiltration drove neurodegeneration. Therapeutic work includes anti-tau antibodies that block aggregate seeding and reduce pathology.

  1. 1
    APOE4 → more tau damageindependent of amyloid

    In tau mice carrying human APOE genes, APOE4 caused markedly more brain shrinkage and inflammation than APOE2 or APOE3, while mice with no APOE were largely protected. In people with amyloid and Alzheimer's symptoms, APOE4 carriers progressed faster.

    Shi et al. 2017 · NatureMouse tauopathy model on human APOE knock-in or knockout backgrounds, plus human imaging cohorts
  2. 2
    Wake ↑ amyloidsleep-wake cycle regulates brain amyloid-β

    Amyloid-β in the fluid around brain cells rose with wakefulness and during sleep deprivation, and fell when the wake-promoting signal orexin was blocked. Chronic sleep restriction increased amyloid plaques in mice, and an orexin receptor blocker reduced them.

    Kang et al. 2009 · ScienceIn vivo brain microdialysis and chronic sleep restriction in amyloid mouse models
  3. 3
    >50%rise in human spinal fluid tau after sleep deprivation

    Tau in mouse brain fluid was about 90% higher during normal wakefulness than sleep and about 100% higher during sleep deprivation. In people, spinal fluid tau rose more than 50% with sleep deprivation, and chronic sleep loss sped the spread of tau pathology in mice.

    Holth et al. 2019 · ScienceMouse microdialysis and tau-spreading model, plus human sleep deprivation with spinal fluid sampling
  4. 4
    Clearance, not productionhow APOE forms change amyloid-β

    In cognitively normal people, amyloid markers varied by APOE type. In mice with human APOE, brain amyloid-β clearance depended on the APOE form even before plaques appeared, while amyloid-β production did not differ.

    Castellano et al. 2011 · Sci Transl MedHuman biomarker cohort plus in vivo microdialysis and stable isotope labeling in mice
  5. 5
    >10×more fibrillar amyloid with APOE4 than APOE3

    Amyloid mice with no APOE had amyloid deposits but almost no damage to surrounding nerve fibers. Adding human APOE produced classic neuritic plaques, and APOE4 mice had more than 10 times as many fibrillar deposits as APOE3 mice.

    Holtzman et al. 2000 · Proc Natl Acad Sci U S AAmyloid precursor protein transgenic mice expressing mouse, human or no APOE
  6. 6
    Astrocyte APOE4 offstrongly protects against tau damage

    Switching off APOE4 in astrocytes after tau pathology had started markedly reduced neurodegeneration and tau pathology, lowered disease-related gene activity in all brain cell types, and reduced microglia eating synapses.

    Wang et al. 2021 · NeuronConditional knockout of astrocyte APOE3 or APOE4 in tau mice, with single-nucleus RNA sequencing
  7. 7
    T cellsdrive tau-related brain loss

    Mice with tau pathology, but not those with only amyloid, developed a distinct immune response with many cytotoxic T cells, also seen in human Alzheimer's brain. Removing microglia or T cells blocked brain shrinkage, and blocking interferon-γ or PD-1 signaling reduced it.

    Chen et al. 2023 · NatureImmune profiling of amyloid and tau mouse models and human Alzheimer's brain tissue
  8. 8
    Microgliadrive APOE-dependent neurodegeneration

    In a tau mouse model, damage caused by microglia, rather than direct toxicity of tau, was the main driver of neurodegeneration, and APOE controlled neurodegeneration mainly by changing how activated microglia became.

    Shi et al. 2019 · J Exp MedMicroglia depletion in tau mice with different APOE genotypes
  9. 9
    Minutes to hourssynaptic activity changes brain amyloid-β

    Brain fluid amyloid-β levels rose and fell with synaptic activity within minutes to hours, driven mainly by release from synaptic vesicles, offering a reason why active brain regions are prone to amyloid build-up.

    Cirrito et al. 2005 · NeuronIn vivo microdialysis with field potential recordings in mice, and brain slice experiments
  10. 10
    Less activity, fewer plaquesneuronal activity sets regional vulnerability

    Before plaques formed, brain regions with higher amyloid-β and lactate, a marker of neuronal activity, later had more plaques. Long-term reduction of whisker input lowered amyloid-β and plaque growth in the matching brain area.

    Bero et al. 2011 · Nat NeurosciMicrodialysis across brain regions and whisker deprivation in amyloid mice
  11. 11
    Ab-APOE, no bleedsantibody to APOE cleared vessel amyloid

    An antibody against aggregated human APOE (HAE-4) reduced amyloid in plaques and in blood vessel walls and improved vessel function, without the microbleeds that an anti-amyloid antibody caused in the same APOE4 mice.

    Xiong et al. 2021 · Sci Transl MedChronic antibody treatment in APOE4 amyloid mice with cerebral amyloid angiopathy
  12. 12
    ~50%APOE4 lowering protected the brain

    Antisense drugs that cut APOE4 protein by about 50% in tau mice protected against tau pathology and neurodegeneration, reduced inflammation, preserved synapses, and lowered the blood marker neurofilament light.

    Litvinchuk et al. 2021 · Ann NeurolIntraventricular antisense oligonucleotide treatment in P301S/APOE4 mice
  13. 13
    Timing mattersfor lowering APOE against amyloid

    Lowering APOE with an antisense drug soon after birth reduced amyloid pathology, but starting when plaques were forming did not change total plaque load, though plaques were larger and caused less nerve damage.

    Huynh et al. 2017 · NeuronAntisense oligonucleotide treatment at two ages in APOE3 and APOE4 amyloid mice
  14. 14
    Aggregated APOEas an antibody target

    An antibody that prefers clumped, lipid-free APOE over the APOE in circulation bound APOE in plaques and reduced amyloid deposition when given into the brain or by injection, an effect that needed the antibody's immune-activating Fc region.

    Liao et al. 2018 · J Clin InvestAntibody discovery and treatment in APOE4 amyloid mice, central and peripheral delivery
  15. 15
    APOE3 Christchurchblocked amyloid-driven tau spread

    Mice carrying the protective APOE3 Christchurch variant, found in a person who resisted inherited Alzheimer's, had much less tau pathology around plaques, with a stronger microglial response and better clearance of tau clumps.

    Chen et al. 2024 · CellNew APOE3ch knock-in mouse crossed to amyloid model, injected with human Alzheimer's tau
  16. 16
    Anti-tau antibodiesreduced tau and improved memory in mice

    Antibodies chosen for blocking tau seeding, given into the brain for 3 months, reduced aggregated tau, microglial activation and seeding activity, and improved cognitive deficits in tau mice.

    Yanamandra et al. 2013 · NeuronThree-month antibody infusion in P301S tau mice
  17. 17
    1,000×rise in blood amyloid-β with a peripheral antibody

    An anti-amyloid antibody (m266) given into the blood bound plasma amyloid-β, raised it about 1,000-fold, and reduced brain amyloid deposits without binding the plaques, suggesting it worked by drawing amyloid-β out of the brain.

    DeMattos et al. 2001 · Proc Natl Acad Sci U S APeripheral antibody treatment in PDAPP amyloid mice
  18. 18
    Lipid effluxeased APOE4-linked tau damage

    In tau mice, APOE4 led to fat build-up and cholesterol and lysosome problems in glial cells. Boosting lipid export with an LXR agonist or extra ABCA1 strongly reduced tau pathology and neurodegeneration.

    Litvinchuk et al. 2024 · NeuronDrug and genetic treatment in P301S tau mice with human APOE4
  19. 19
    Gut microbesinfluence tau-related brain damage

    Raising tau mice with human APOE germ-free, or altering their gut microbes with antibiotics, reduced gliosis, tau pathology and neurodegeneration, with effects that depended on sex and APOE type.

    Seo et al. 2023 · ScienceGerm-free and antibiotic-treated tau mice expressing human APOE isoforms
  20. 20
    TREM2 loss protectsin tau mice, unlike in amyloid

    Tau mice lacking the microglial receptor TREM2 had less brain shrinkage and less microglial activation by 9 months, though tau pathology itself was unchanged.

    Leyns et al. 2017 · Proc Natl Acad Sci U S ATrem2 knockout crossed to PS19 tau mice
  21. 21
    TREM2lets microglia sense lipids and surround plaques

    Without TREM2, microglia failed to cluster around amyloid plaques and amyloid built up. TREM2 senses lipids exposed on damaged neurons and amyloid, and the Alzheimer's risk variant R47H weakens this sensing.

    Wang et al. 2015 · CellTREM2-deficient 5XFAD amyloid mice and lipid-binding assays
  22. 22
    Deep sleepdisruption raises amyloid-β the next morning

    In 17 healthy adults, specifically disrupting slow-wave sleep for one night raised spinal fluid amyloid-β40, and poorer sleep at home over the prior six nights was linked to higher tau.

    Ju et al. 2017 · BrainWithin-person sleep disruption versus sham study, 17 adults aged 35-65
  23. 23
    80.4% vs 83.7%sleep efficiency with versus without amyloid

    Among 142 cognitively normal adults, those with amyloid build-up spent less of their time in bed asleep and napped more often, while total sleep time did not differ.

    Ju et al. 2013 · JAMA NeurolCross-sectional study with 2 weeks of actigraphy, 142 participants
  24. 24
    1-2 Hzslow-wave sleep tracks tau

    In mostly cognitively normal older adults wearing a forehead EEG device, less slow-wave activity in deep sleep, especially at 1 to 2 Hz, was linked to Alzheimer's pathology, most strongly tau.

    Lucey et al. 2019 · Sci Transl MedSingle-channel EEG sleep monitoring with PET and spinal fluid biomarkers in aging cohorts
  25. 25
    Plaques disrupt sleepand clearing them restored it

    After plaques formed in amyloid mice, the sleep-wake cycle deteriorated and the daily rise and fall of brain amyloid-β disappeared, also seen in young adults with presenilin mutations. Removing plaques by immunization restored both.

    Roh et al. 2012 · Sci Transl MedAmyloid mice with sleep recordings and microdialysis, plus spinal fluid sampling in mutation carriers
  26. 26
    25 yearsbefore symptoms, amyloid changes begin in inherited AD

    In 128 members of families with inherited Alzheimer's, spinal fluid amyloid-β42 began to fall about 25 years before expected symptoms, amyloid plaques and tau rose about 15 years before, and memory problems appeared about 10 years before.

    Bateman et al. 2012 · N Engl J MedDIAN prospective cohort, 128 participants
